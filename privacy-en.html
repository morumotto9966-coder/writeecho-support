WRITEECHO PRIVACY

Privacy policy

Your notes stay on your device. Here is how information is handled when you use the app, make a purchase, share your work or contact support.

Last updated and effective: October 5, 2026
Applies to WriteEcho / 書寫聲影 version 1.0

01 / SCOPE

Provider and approach

This policy applies to WriteEcho: Handwritten Notes, also named 書寫聲影－手寫影音筆記 (the app), and this support website, provided by WU GUEI-RONG. Contact: morumotto9966@gmail.com.

The app has no developer-operated account registration system or cloud server for your notes. Core handwriting, import and video processing take place on your device. Notes, backgrounds and microphone recordings are not automatically uploaded to the developer.

On-device processing does not mean no information is ever processed. Apple purchase services, sharing services you select, website hosting and support email have separate information flows described below.

02 / NOTES AND IMPORTS

Content you create or select

  • Projects and notes: project names, update times, pages, stroke coordinates and appearance, text, shapes, paper and background settings are used to display, edit and reopen your work.
  • Images and PDFs: the app reads and processes files you choose and stores page backgrounds. PDF parsing and image processing happen on your device, not on a developer-operated conversion server.
  • Local settings and indexes: necessary preferences, project and video-draft indexes, and cached status used for display are stored locally. The app uses WebView IndexedDB/local storage and device file storage to provide its features.

You select files or photos through system interfaces. If you choose a file from a cloud provider, the operating system and that service manage its download and associated information processing.

03 / PERMISSIONS AND VIDEO

Microphone access is for recording you choose

The app captures audio only when you choose whiteboard recording with audio and grant microphone access. Audio is combined with the whiteboard video. Silent recording does not use the microphone. Denying access does not prevent ordinary handwriting or silent recording.

Whiteboard recording is not camera recording and does not record other apps' screens. Temporary segments may be written to device storage while recording; the microphone track is closed when recording stops. Video sources, edit settings and drafts may remain on the device for later editing and are managed separately from disposable sharing or processing files.

You may stop recording or withdraw microphone permission in iOS/iPadOS Settings. Withdrawing permission does not delete earlier recordings. Delete existing drafts or exported files separately.

04 / IN-APP PURCHASES

Apple handles payment and purchase verification

The full version is a one-time, non-consumable in-app purchase. Through Apple StoreKit, the app obtains product details, prices, verified transactions and current purchase entitlement to enable features, restore purchases and handle cancelled or revoked purchase states.

The app may cache purchase status locally for display, but entitlement remains based on Apple's verification results. The app does not ask you to give the developer your Apple Account password, payment-card number or full payment details. It does not send receipts to a developer-operated verification server.

Apple processes store account, transaction and payment information and may provide sales or financial reports to the developer under its service terms. Apple's processing is subject to the Apple Privacy Policy. Restoring purchases does not upload or restore notes.

05 / CONNECTIONS AND SHARING

You choose whether to use other services

  • This version has no third-party advertising SDK, behavioral analytics tracking service or cross-app/site tracking feature. It does not sell your notes, recordings or imported content.
  • Code, fonts, icons and PDF components are packaged with the app; loading these assets does not require connections to third-party content delivery networks.
  • Purchases, restoration and product-information queries may connect to Apple. These are separate from core on-device features.
  • When you choose the system share sheet, send email, save to a cloud location or open an external link, selected content or ordinary network information is provided to the relevant service for that action. Your selected provider manages its subsequent handling.

Third-party software notices can be read offline in the app. Using an open-source component for on-device processing does not mean its authors receive your notes.

06 / RETENTION AND DELETION

Local work and exported copies

  • Projects, pages, backgrounds and video drafts remain on the current device until you use the corresponding delete controls, clear app data or remove the app. The developer does not provide a cloud copy of your notes.
  • You can delete individual pages, whole projects or video drafts. Deleting a page or project does not automatically delete exported files or separately retained video drafts.
  • Temporary sharing and processing files are handled according to task completion and system cleanup. Unfinished work, source videos or editing drafts may continue to use storage.
  • PDFs, PNGs, videos and shared copies saved in Photos, Files, cloud storage or another app must be managed or deleted in that location. Apple transaction records follow Apple's retention rules; removing the app does not delete the transaction.
  • If you enable system backup or device migration, retention and transfer depend on system settings and provider policies, rather than an app-provided sync feature.

Export important work in advance. Because the developer does not hold a copy of your local notes, we cannot recover or remotely delete them from a server.

07 / SUPPORT EMAIL

Information you send voluntarily

If you email support, we receive your email address, message and any attachments you choose to send, such as device details, app version, issue screenshots or transaction information. We use this information to answer questions, handle purchase or privacy requests and troubleshoot issues, not for advertising tracking.

Support correspondence is handled through email services. Our current contact address uses Gmail, whose provider processes email under the Google Privacy Policy. Please provide only what is needed and redact unrelated personal information.

Support correspondence is retained only for handling requests, necessary follow-up, disputes or legal retention needs. Information no longer needed for those purposes is removed. You may request access, correction or deletion by email, subject to exceptions for records that must be retained by law.

08 / THIS WEBSITE

Website processing is separate from app content

This public website is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may process IP addresses, browser information, request times and access logs for delivery, security and operation. See the GitHub Privacy Statement.

We have not added ads, analytics trackers, third-party embedded content or visitor upload forms to this website. It does not set its own cookies or use browser local storage to track visitors. Fonts use your system fonts; website images and styles come from this site. Hosting logs do not give the developer access to your notes inside the app.

09 / YOUR CHOICES

Privacy requests and security

You may choose not to import files, record audio, share or purchase. You can withdraw system permissions and delete local content. Depending on applicable law in your location, you may have rights to access, correct, delete or otherwise control support information we hold. Email us to make a request; we will handle it under applicable requirements. We may reasonably verify the requester's identity, but will not ask for Apple passwords or payment verification codes.

On-device processing and limited permissions reduce exposure, but no electronic storage or transmission is absolutely secure. Protect your device, backups and sharing choices. Minors should ask a parent or guardian to assist with purchases or support requests involving personal information.

10 / UPDATES

Policy changes and contact

We will update this policy and its date when app features or information practices change. Material changes affecting users will be communicated appropriately through the app, release information or this website.
