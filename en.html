01 / GETTING STARTED

Create, write and export

  1. Create a project

    On the project home screen, choose 新增專案筆記 (New project), name it and choose a background. Tap a project card to reopen it. Use 頁面與紙張 (Pages and paper) to add pages or change paper styles.

  2. Write and draw

    Choose a pen, then adjust its color and width. Use the eraser, undo and redo as needed. Some advanced pen styles and shapes require the full version.

  3. Import an image or PDF

    Open 頁面與紙張 → 匯入圖片／PDF. An image becomes a page background; the full version inserts pages from a multi-page PDF. Use 移動底圖 (Move background) to reposition it.

  4. Record your whiteboard

    Choose 只錄白板畫面 for silent recording, or 錄影並錄音 to include microphone audio. After recording, edit the clip, crop the frame and export an MP4. Stay in the editor while export processing finishes.

  5. Export your notes

    In Pages and paper, choose 匯出整本／選頁筆記. Select pages, the content area and PDF or PNG, then save using the system share sheet. Multiple PNG images are packaged into a ZIP file.

To leave the whiteboard, open 更多功能 (More) and return to 專案首頁 (Project home) so the app can finish saving. The app's 使用說明 (User guide) is also available offline.

03 / PURCHASES

Purchased, but features are still locked?

  1. Make sure the App Store is signed in to the Apple Account originally used to purchase the full version.
  2. Connect to the internet and open the full-version purchase screen in the app.
  3. Tap 恢復購買 (Restore purchases) and wait for the App Store to confirm your purchase.

If access is not restored, check that the transaction was completed rather than pending, cancelled or refunded. Do not repeatedly pay to troubleshoot a purchase; contact support below.

Restoring purchases restores feature access, not notes, backgrounds or videos. It does not transfer projects from another device.

04 / YOUR WORK

Saved on your device. Export what matters.

  • Projects, handwriting, pages and backgrounds are stored on the current device. The app does not provide automatic cross-device sync or developer-hosted cloud backups.
  • Export important notes as PDF or PNG and videos as MP4, then save them to a safe location of your choice.
  • These exports are readable or shareable results, not project backups that restore every editable stroke and setting.
  • 刪除目前頁 (Delete current page) removes that page's strokes and background. At least one page must remain. Save any needed content before deleting a project.
  • Removing the app or clearing its data may remove local work. System backups and device migration depend on Apple services and your settings; they are not an app-provided restore feature.

If you see a warning that your work has not been safely saved, keep the current screen open, try to export important readable content, and check free storage. Do not uninstall the app or clear its data first. Contact support with a screenshot of the error.

05 / COMMON QUESTIONS

A few quick answers

Why is import locked, or only one PDF page visible?

The free version can import one image or the first page of a PDF on project page 2. Create and switch to page 2 first. Multi-page PDFs and additional imports require the full version.

How do I remove an unwanted PDF page?

Switch to that page, open Pages and paper, choose 刪除目前頁 (Delete current page), and confirm. Remove unwanted PDF pages individually. The only remaining page cannot be deleted.

Some strokes are out of view after rotating my iPad.

Rotation changes the visible area. Use 移動畫布 (Pan canvas) to see content outside the screen. 畫布回到中央 changes the viewing position; 底圖回到中央 resets the imported background's position and size. These are different actions.

Why did PNG export create a ZIP?

Multiple pages or large split exports create multiple PNG images, which are packaged into a ZIP. Choose Save to Files in the share sheet, then tap the ZIP in the Files app and open the extracted folder.

Why is microphone recording silent or unavailable?

Choose 錄影並錄音 and allow microphone access for this app in iPhone or iPad Settings. You can choose silent whiteboard recording instead if you do not want to grant access. Also check playback volume and the audio output device.

Will restoring a purchase recover my old notes?

No. Purchase access and local notes are separate. There is no developer-hosted cloud copy of your notes. Export important work in advance, and do not delete the app as a first step when investigating a saving problem.
